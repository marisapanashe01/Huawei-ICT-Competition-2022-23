## ✦ 01 — The Farewell

### 16 May 2023 · Tongfu Restaurant, Zimbabwe

Before China, there was the send-off.

On 16 May 2023, we gathered at Tongfu Restaurant for a farewell dinner ahead of the Huawei ICT Competition 2022–2023 Global Final. It was a chance to celebrate the journey so far and acknowledge the mentors, officials, and institutions supporting our team.

Among those present were our University of Zimbabwe instructor, **Mr Bernard Mapako**, and our Huawei patron and guide, **Mr Tafadzwa Muriritirwa**. Representatives associated with Huawei and the university attended, and members of *The Herald* news team were also present.

One of the day's most memorable photographs shows Team Zimbabwe holding the Zimbabwean and Chinese flags side by side with officials—a fitting symbol of the international exchange that lay ahead.

## ✦ 02 — The News That Made It Real

### Qualifying for the Global Final

We still remember the excitement of receiving the results confirming that we had qualified for the Global Final. What had started as a competition had become an opportunity to represent Zimbabwe internationally.

The official invitation letters followed. One line captured the spirit of the event:

> “Let's build connections, share the glory, and create a brighter future with ICT.”

The invitation made the journey feel real. Shenzhen was no longer just a destination on paper—it was where we would test our skills, meet students from around the world, and experience the technology industry on a different scale.

## ✦ 05 — Global Final Examination

### 24 May 2023 · Huawei Industrial Base, J1 Main Building, Bantian, Shenzhen

Before entering the examination, we posed for a team photograph in front of the “Huawei ICT Competition 2022–2023 Global Final” banner. Mr Tafadzwa Muriritirwa took the picture—a small moment that became part of our history.

The examination lasted approximately seven to eight hours. It demanded concentration, technical knowledge, and problem-solving. By the end, we were exhausted, relieved, and happy to have completed the challenge we had travelled so far to face.

Afterwards, we had dinner and walked around the J1 Main Building area, finally able to breathe after an intense day.

## ✦ 06 — Discovering Shenzhen

### 25–26 May 2023

After the examination, we explored Shenzhen: its streets, buildings, train stations, public spaces, and everyday rhythm. We boarded a train and ended up in another part of the city, turning a simple outing into an unexpected adventure.

We returned to the Huawei base for lunch with the other teams—crab included—and later visited Huawei's headquarters and the Ox Horn Campus in Guangdong.

We spent more time exploring the city the following day, visiting malls and shops, looking at clothes, shoes, and gadgets, trying Chinese orange juice, and photographing the surrounding buildings and scenery. These little discoveries became part of the experience too.

## ✦ 07 — Huawei's Ox Horn Campus

The Ox Horn Campus left a strong impression on us. Its buildings drew inspiration from European architectural styles, with areas resembling places such as Paris, Germany, and England. A train system connected different parts of the extensive campus.

We also took a group photograph with Chinese and Latin American students we had met during the competition. And after a full day of tours? A small shopping trip and a familiar stop at KFC. Even on a technology journey, sometimes you just want something familiar to eat.

## ✦ 09 — The Award & Closing Ceremony

Some of the friends we had made received major prizes in the Innovation Track, and we were proud to celebrate their achievements. Team Zimbabwe also took to the stage alongside participants from countries including Thailand, Singapore, Chile, Italy, and Ethiopia as we received our medals.

**Team Zimbabwe placed third globally in the Network Track.**

It was a moment of pride—not only for the result, but for the preparation, teamwork, and persistence behind it. The photographs from that day capture the smiles and excitement of reaching the end of a demanding international competition.

## ✦ 10 — Beijing: An Unexpected Extension

### 28–30 May 2023 · Beijing, China

While many teams began travelling home, Team Zimbabwe stayed a little longer. We were informed that the then Minister of Foreign Affairs and International Trade, **Hon. Frederick Shava**, wished to meet us during his visit.

We travelled to Beijing and visited a Huawei research and development facility in the Chaoyang area. We met the minister and members of his delegation, participated in media interviews, and took a group photograph. Afterwards, we enjoyed a Beijing roast-duck dinner before preparing to leave China.

On 30 May, we headed to the airport and began our journey home.
